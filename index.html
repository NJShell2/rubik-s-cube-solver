    Reading the notation

    • U D L R F B — Up, Down, Left, Right, Front, Back face, turned clockwise.
    • R′ (say “R prime”) — same face, counter-clockwise.
    • U2 — a half turn, direction doesn’t matter.
    • Uw / Rw — a wide turn: the outer two layers move together. Essential on 4×4 and 5×5.
    • Algorithms are just recipes. Do them slowly and exactly — the coach re-checks the cube when you’re done.
    White on top, green in front is the home position the coach assumes. Big cubes (4×4, 5×5) use the reduction method: fix the centers, pair the edges, then it solves exactly like a 3×3.
